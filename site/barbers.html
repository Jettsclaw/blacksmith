Meet the chairs.

From head barbers to academy apprentices coming up the right way — every chair at Blacksmith is held to one standard.

Head of Walk-Ins

Bayli

@bayli_barber

On the floor Monday to Friday from 9am and always on walk-ins. Easy going, meticulous and great with every hairstyle — a fade and texture specialist who reads a face before she picks up the clippers. Can't make it in during the day? Book Bayli after hours in our booking chat.

Manager · Head Barber

Jarred

@rodneyblade_

Runs the shop, and in the chair mainly Wednesday to Sunday — you might see him floating around on his days off too. Experienced and cool under pressure, a master of the blade and beard, and brilliant with kids. Fussy regulars love him for it.

Book with Jarred
Barber

Ben

@the_suited_barber

Scissor work on another level. Classic cuts, long hair and proper styling are his thing — and he's the man for a big change. Precise, efficient, fully equipped, and genuinely good company in the chair.

Book with Ben
Barber

Mubarak

@mubacutshair

Fast hands, sharp results. Fades and curly hair are his specialty, and he keeps it moving without ever cutting corners. An easy, friendly chair — a favourite with teenagers and younger blokes.

Apprentice

Barber

Blackrose Stylist

Sammi

@blackrose.salonstudio

Runs Blackrose, the salon side of Blacksmith — a private, secluded space right next door, away from the busy barber floor. A true all-rounder: colour, fades and scissor work all in safe hands. Ideal if you'd prefer a quieter chair, and a favourite for couples coming in together.
